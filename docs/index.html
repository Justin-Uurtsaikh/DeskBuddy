LOCAL-FIRST BY DESIGN

Your buddy never leaves your desktop.

Your sprite artwork is stored in DeskBuddy’s private app-data folder. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts, API keys, cloud generation, or AI services involved.

QUICK QUESTIONS

The tiny details.

What kind of image do I need?+

Use a clean transparent PNG sprite sheet with equal square cells. Screenshots, white backgrounds, and fake checkerboard backgrounds will not work.

Can I draw inside DeskBuddy?+

Yes. The built-in drawing mode includes a transparent canvas, brush, eraser, multiple frames, and an optional onion-skin reference.

Can I have more than one buddy?+

Yes. Save and launch multiple buddies, and each one can wander on its own staggered schedule.

Where is my artwork stored?+

DeskBuddy copies your sprites into its private local app-data folder. It does not upload them anywhere.

Which computers are supported?+

The source is configured for macOS 13 or later on Intel and Apple silicon, plus 64-bit Windows. Running from source requires Node.js 22.12 or later.
