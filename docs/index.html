NOTES FROM THE WORKBENCH

A few things Sprout gets asked.

What kind of image should I use?+

A transparent PNG sprite sheet with equal square cells. Avoid screenshots, white backgrounds, and fake checkerboards.

Can I draw inside DeskBuddy?+

Yes. The app includes a transparent canvas, brush, eraser, multiple frames, and an optional onion skin.

Can I keep more than one buddy?+

Yes. Each saved buddy can be shown, hidden, or deleted from your collection.

Which computers are supported?+

The project supports macOS 13 or later on Intel and Apple silicon, plus 64-bit Windows.
