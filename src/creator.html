YOUR LITTLE DESKTOP COMPANION

Build a sprite buddy that moves with your own animation.

Import a clean sprite sheet or draw each frame yourself. DeskBuddy plays your idle frames while it rests and your walk frames while it glides around the desktop.

01

Make a buddy

Use a transparent PNG sprite sheet

Use a clean transparent sheet with equal cells. After upload, pick the cell grid that keeps one complete character in each box, then choose each animation's row and start column.

Tip: use the same row for idle and walk if your sheet only has one animation.

Draw your animation frames

Make at least one visible idle frame and one visible walk frame. Add more frames to make a loop.

Idle · frame 1 of 1

The drawing canvas starts transparent, so your finished buddy will have no white box around it.
